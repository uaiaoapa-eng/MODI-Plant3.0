## Brand and distribution notes

- Use the supplied `logo.svg` unchanged. Do not recolor, distort, crop, separate, redraw, or replace either the MODI symbol or the Planet wordmark. Preserve its aspect ratio and built-in colors.
- Public availability of these files is not a standalone trademark or redistribution license. No public MODI Planet CI/BI usage guide was found during source verification.
- These copies are bundled to make the local prototype deterministic and to avoid production hotlinking. Confirm LUXROBO/MODI brand and distribution permission before any public deployment, external redistribution, or third-party use.
- Pretendard is a third-party typeface. Preserve and comply with its applicable font license and notices when distributing it.
